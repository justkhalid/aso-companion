ASO CLUB KIT GENERATOR
Open index.html in Chrome (double-click). Nothing is uploaded except what you post.

Menu
 Clubs      All clubs from the ASO Companion app, with a live story preview. Open, Download or Post each one.
 Events     Same for events.
 Create     Edit one item, or make a new club or event.
 Settings   Sync from the ASO Companion, Facebook/Instagram connection, backup.

Schedules come from the ASO Companion (github.com/justkhalid/aso-companion). Change them there, then press Sync now.
Looks, icons, captions and posters you set here are saved in this browser. Use Backup to move them to another computer.
